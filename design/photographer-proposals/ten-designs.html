Recommended starting design: Golden Hour.

It gives the existing wedding content a clear narrative, preserves portrait photographs and offers an uncomplicated enquiry path. Editorial / Folio is the premium flagship; Gallery Wall suits studios with larger mixed collections.

Visual verification: 320 content/viewport cases; desktop 1440, tablet 768, mobile 390 and narrow 320. All ten designs have complete desktop and mobile screenshots below.

Publishing is deliberate.

Save a private draft, review it at desktop/tablet/mobile sizes, then publish. Existing public URLs remain stable. Old portfolios use their original renderer until explicitly published as version 3. Both version 1 and version 2 portfolios retain their original renderer until explicit republication. Version history restores into a draft; it never silently replaces the public site.
