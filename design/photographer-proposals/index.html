Verified before designing

  • All ten designs currently share one V2 composition. Editorial has its own tokens but no editorial layout, and still displays a fallback notice.
  • PostgreSQL through an async JSONB adapter; collection-style methods do not mean MongoDB.
  • Real lead endpoint: POST /api/public/photographer/{slug}/enquiry. No availability checker or notification dispatch in that path.
  • No draft/published snapshots. Public portfolio GET does not enforce the stored publish flag.
  • Old IDs, current entitlements, public URLs, metadata and content need a versioned, opt-in migration.
